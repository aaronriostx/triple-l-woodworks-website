A LITTLE CLARITY BEFORE YOU START

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Planning custom cabinetry comes with questions. Here are a few helpful starting points for your project.

How do I get started?

Visit the contact page and share a little about the cabinetry you have in mind. Photos of your space, your location, and any inspiration images are a helpful place to start.

What should I have ready before reaching out?

Bring whatever you have: approximate dimensions, photos, ideas for the layout, and preferred materials or finishes. An ideal timeline and budget range also help frame the conversation. You do not need a finished design to make an initial inquiry.

How much does custom cabinetry cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, dimensions, materials, finish, and details of the project. Get in touch to discuss your ideas and the information needed for a project-specific estimate.

How long will my project take?

Timing depends on the design, material availability, project size, and current schedule. Share your target date when you reach out so availability and next steps can be discussed before you make plans.

Can I share inspiration photos or sketches?

Absolutely. Photos, sketches, and examples from the project gallery can help explain the style and features you like. Include what appeals to you about each example, such as the layout, wood tone, or hardware.

Which materials and finishes should I choose?

The right choice depends on how you use the space, the look you want, and your budget. Share your preferences and any practical priorities, such as storage needs or ease of cleaning, when discussing the project.

Do you work in my area?

Include your city or ZIP code in your inquiry so service availability can be confirmed for your location. Service-area details will also be listed on the contact page.

How are measurements and installation handled?

Ask about measurement and installation arrangements when discussing your project. Confirm who is responsible for final measurements, site preparation, delivery, and installation before proceeding. Any measurements you send initially can be approximate.
